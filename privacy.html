Privacy Policy

Project29 · Effective 16 November 2026

Short version: Project29 has no accounts, ads, analytics or in-app purchases. We don't collect your personal information. The only data that leaves your device is anonymous crash and performance data sent to Unity, the engine the game runs on.

What we collect

Nothing. We, Ugly Cat Games, don't receive or store any information about you. The game doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and it doesn't use your camera or microphone.

What stays on your device

Your save progress and settings (such as volume) are stored locally on your device. We can't see them. Deleting the game deletes them.

Unity crash and performance data

Project29 is made with the Unity engine. Unity automatically collects anonymous diagnostic data when the game runs or crashes, such as the device model, operating system version, crash reports and performance figures. Unity uses this to find and fix engine problems.

This data isn't linked to your identity, and neither we nor Unity use it to track you or show you ads. Unity handles it under its own privacy policy.

Tracking

We don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Children

Project29 is suitable for all ages. Because we don't collect personal information from anyone, we don't knowingly collect it from children under 13 either.

Your choices

Since we hold no data about you, there's nothing for us to delete or export. To remove everything the game stores on your device, delete the app. For questions about the diagnostic data Unity holds, see Unity's privacy policy linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email team.uglycat@gmail.com or see our contact page.
